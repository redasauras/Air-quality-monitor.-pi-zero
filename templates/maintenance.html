Maintenance checklist

Practical checks
Periodically inspect sensor openings

Keep the PMSA003I air inlet and outlet clear. Keep the assembly away from fabric, walls, dust buildup, and anything that blocks airflow.

Check wiring and power

Inspect the I²C connections and power leads for loose plugs, damaged insulation, or unusual heat. Shut the Pi down before changing wiring.

Keep the sensor dry

Avoid condensation, water, cooking steam, and very damp locations. Do not spray cleaner into the sensor or open the module unless its manufacturer instructions specifically call for it.

Compare readings occasionally

Compare against a trusted nearby monitor under similar conditions. Different sensor models and locations can disagree; do not use a single comparison as proof of exact accuracy.

Review fan / sensor status

Watch for sensor errors or readings that become stuck. Check the manufacturer's documentation before attempting any internal cleaning or fan service.
