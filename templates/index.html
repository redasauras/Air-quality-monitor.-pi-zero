Graph lines show actual saved samples; optional dashed overlays are rolling averages calculated for viewing only. Long-range charts may sample selected stored points to stay responsive, but summaries and CSV exports use all readings in the selected time range. No historic database values are rewritten. US customary display units: °F and inHg. Particle concentrations remain in µg/m³, the standard unit. PM2.5 AQI is an approximate estimate, not a certified regulatory measurement. Advice is based only on indoor sensors: the device cannot measure outdoor temperature, outdoor pollution, CO₂, VOCs, or actual ventilation. Check outdoor conditions before opening or closing windows. Keep the sensor away from direct sunlight, vents, cooking steam, and dusty surfaces for representative readings.
